ABOUT KLENZIX HYDERABAD

Engineering Superior Cleanliness with State-of-the-Art Technology

Based in Hyderabad, Telangana, Klenzix operates one of the region's most advanced hygiene product formulation plants. Our state-of-the-art facility is equipped with automated blending, filling, and quality-testing lines that ensure consistent output at scale. Every batch undergoes rigorous lab analysis for pH balance, viscosity, and antibacterial efficacy before dispatch.

Founded with the vision to make premium cleaning products accessible across India, Klenzix combines cutting-edge chemistry with responsible manufacturing. We source high-grade surfactants, fragrances, and preservatives to deliver formulations that are both powerful on grime and gentle on surfaces.
